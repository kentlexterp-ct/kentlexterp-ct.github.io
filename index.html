Problem & context

Invoices, receipts, leads, and support tickets need different handling. The service turns PDF or text input into a structured classification and routing decision.

Constraints

06 / BEFORE WE TALK

A few useful answers.

Are you looking for a job or freelance projects?

Both. I’m interested in remote junior or mid-level Python roles that match my experience, and scoped automation work for businesses and agencies.

What kinds of projects are a good fit?

Python APIs, integrations between existing tools, AI-assisted workflows, and GoHighLevel automation. A good starting project has a specific input, expected output, and a person who can confirm the result is useful.

Can you work with my existing CRM or tools?

That depends on their APIs, webhook support, and access limits. Share the tools and steps you want connected; I’ll review feasibility before scope is agreed.

How do you scope cost and timeline?

After reviewing the workflow, integrations, and acceptance criteria. I don’t publish a fixed turnaround or universal rate because requirements differ.

Will AI handle every decision automatically?

Only where appropriate. We should define evaluation examples and decide which outputs need human review. A demo alone is not evidence of classification accuracy.
