About Me

Support That Goes Beyond Basic VA Work

I’m a Technical Virtual Assistant focused on CRM, research, admin support, and AI automation. I help businesses stay organized, maintain accurate information, manage leads, and handle repetitive day-to-day tasks. My goal is to make your workload easier to manage without adding unnecessary complexity.

I work best with clear priorities, organized processes, and open communication. I document my work, keep information structured, and use AI tools to research and solve problems efficiently. When something can be improved through better systems or automation, I look for a practical way to handle it.

What separates me from a traditional VA is my technical background in Python, FastAPI, REST APIs, and API integrations. This allows me to handle technical tasks and troubleshoot workflow problems that may normally need to be escalated. I work best with small business owners, founders, executives, agency owners, and coaches who need dependable support behind the scenes.
